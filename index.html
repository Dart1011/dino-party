HOW TO PLAY

Outrun the whole crew

  1. Create and shareOne player creates a room and sends the invite link. Friends open it, choose a name, and join.
  2. Jump and duckJump over cacti and low pterodactyls. Duck under high pterodactyls. While airborne, duck to land faster.
  3. Run endlesslyThere is no finish line or time limit. Your speed increases over time, making obstacles harder to avoid. Day alternates with night as you run. You have three lives; when every runner is out, the greatest distance wins.
